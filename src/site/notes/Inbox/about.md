# About

This is the digital garden of **Nexus** — a teacher, artist, and technologist exploring the intersections of AI, education, and social change.

## What I do

I teach visual arts and AI to VMBO students at SKVR, Accent Avondschool, and VCH in the Netherlands. My work focuses on making technology accessible — especially for students who are often told technology is not for them.

## What this garden is

A collection of notes, ideas, and connections. Not finished work — thinking in public. Topics include:

- AI and education — how students learn with and about AI
- Cultural theory — Sara Ahmed, bell hooks, Gloria Wekker
- Technology infrastructure — local AI, open source, digital sovereignty
- Teaching practice — what happens in the classroom

## Why a digital garden?

Blogs are polished. Gardens grow. This is where I plant ideas and see what takes root.
